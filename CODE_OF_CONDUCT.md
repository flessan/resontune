# Code of Conduct

ResonTune exists to make music more accessible. The community around it
should feel like a good record store: welcoming, curious, and generous.

## Expectations

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before accusing.
- Credit creators. Never submit music you don't have the rights to.
- Keep spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) on topic and harassment-free.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, doxxing, sexualized content in project spaces,
sustained disruption, and submitting infringing or malicious content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior that violates this
code. Report incidents privately to the maintainers.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
